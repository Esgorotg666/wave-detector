Important limitations: This PWA can only monitor while the app is open and in the foreground. iOS does not allow continuous background sensor access for web apps. The magnetometer data available via DeviceOrientation is limited (not raw µT values). It can detect vibration/motion and some audio energy, but cannot reliably detect electric fields, RF, or sophisticated directed devices. For better magnetic readings, use a native magnetometer app. If you feel persistent sensations, consider seeing a doctor to rule out medical causes.
